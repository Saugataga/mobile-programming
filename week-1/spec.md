# Paila Paid Micro-Projects for Nepali Students 

## Problem statement
Many Nepali students have valuable skills but struggle to find paid opportunities because most employers require previous professional experience. Meanwhile, small businesses need affordable support for short-term tasks but cannot always hire full-time employees or professional agencies. There is currently a lack of a trusted platform that effectively connects these students with suitable paid projects.

## Objective
To develop a reliable mobile platform that connects Nepali students with short-term paid projects from local businesses, enabling students to earn income, gain practical experience and build verified portfolios while helping businesses access affordable talent.
